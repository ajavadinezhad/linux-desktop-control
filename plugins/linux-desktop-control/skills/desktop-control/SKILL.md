Consent-first orchestration is mandatory. Those channels bypass the intended desktop-consent workflow even though they do not breach the portal. After consent, launch and operate apps only through the observed GUI. No preparatory test windows before consent. Denied/cancelled consent means no app launch. examples/consent_first.py demonstrates the ordering without an app command runner.

Every desktop_action requires window_observation_id from the inspected current screenshot. The GNOME observer must provide protocol 2 and fresh pointer snapshots (helper revision 3). Input is refused if the observer is unavailable or the observation expired, was used, changed window/geometry/context epoch or was invalidated by closure. Focus away and back still advances the epoch. Target checks run before input events, throughout interruptible waits and around image publication; raw releases remain possible after target loss. By default, unexpected loss cancels the remaining batch, releases held virtual input and closes sharing, invalidating queued work. The explicit retained-sharing mouse-motion exception below grants no focus reclaim or action replay. Wait for the user's explicit new readiness before starting a new consented session.

An intentional app-launch/window transition is a separate focus_transition=true action, restricted to one left click or a Super/Return/Ctrl+s/Ctrl+Shift+s/Ctrl+o gesture. No text, scrolling, dragging or remaining sequence is permitted in that mode. Inspect the returned image before any further action. A transition authorizes that one gesture; it cannot distinguish a simultaneous human switch and is not an automatic focus-reclaim mechanism. Ordinary batches are strictly fenced. Window moves/resizes and cross-window drags can therefore be conservatively refused/interrupted. Use short observed steps.

The guard detects window/context loss, not physical-input provenance. Activity that leaves window identity, geometry and observed Shell context unchanged may remain undetected, including pointer movement or focus changes inside one app unless an optional exact accessibility identity detects them. No atomic guarantee exists between a foreground check and OS event delivery; already delivered effects cannot be undone. Native public system-window/context signals are read-only. Already published images cannot be retracted. Do not restart sharing automatically after interruption.

Capture accepts recent frames with a settling fence.
